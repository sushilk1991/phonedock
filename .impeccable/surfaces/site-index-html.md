## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the stage seen from the booth. Phones stand on their marks under a light rig while an agent calls the show, and the one step it can't do is handed to you by a cue light. It refuses the category page: a dark gradient hero with a terminal mockup over a grid of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Backstage stage management, never front of house (no curtains, masks, velvet or marquee bulbs). Show mode (dark): matte stage black tinted indigo, cool-white light pools and faint haze beams. Work-light mode (light): the app's pale stage grey (#E8E8EC) with white prompt-book paper and graphite ink. Saturated ink is only spike tape and cue lights, in the app's control colours: agent purple, you indigo, needs-you orange. Archivo: expanded heavy for headlines, condensed caps for cue and state labels. JetBrains Mono only for commands and flow YAML. A prompt-book page is a ruled page with a call margin carrying real calls (tool names, steps), and pencil brackets drawn as SVG. Buttons are cue-light keys.
- Raise (ikebana ma, declined): charged emptiness. Each section has one primary, one secondary and one supporting element, and the empty stage is kept, not filled.
- Raise (signboard alley, declined): illumination is state. Only what is live is lit, and idle things sit unlit.
- Raise (op-art textile, declined): total palette commitment. Ground and ink, plus the three state colours, and nothing else.
- Raise (Łowicz papercut, declined): depth comes from overlap and fog, never from drop-shadowed 2D cards.

STORY: The visitor watches an agent drive phones live, sees it stop and ask for a person, and presses Take Over and Done themselves. They understand agent ↔ PhoneDock ↔ devices, see four targets and any MCP agent, trust the plain limits, then download or copy setup.
