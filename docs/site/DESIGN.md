# Design System: PhoneDock site

This file covers the public landing page in `site/` only. The Mac app's design guide is `design/DESIGN.md`.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Stage Seen from the Booth"**

The page is a stage manager's view of a show that really happened. A plain black phone plays real screen recordings while, beside it, a dark terminal card titled Claude Code prints the agent's real calls as they land in the clip and ends on its real answer. Dark mode is show mode: matte black tinted indigo, cool light pools, film grain over a defocused night desk. Light mode is work-light mode: the app's pale stage grey with white paper and graphite ink. Both themes come from one set of `light-dark()` custom properties on `:root`, and three show bands (the hero, the use cases and the handoff) stay dark in both.

Saturated colour belongs to state. The three control colours from the Mac app (agent purple, you indigo, needs-you orange) mark who is in control, and the accent indigo fills the primary key. Everything else is ground, paper, ink, hairline rules and booth light. Sections are long ruled lists and column grids on the bare ground, with very few boxed containers. Real footage carries every claim: the hero reel, the four use-case runs, the handoff recording and a frame of the real PhoneDock window. AI-generated stills appear only as mood, defocused, scrimmed or masked, and are credited in the footer.

Motion is gentle and always optional. The typed prompt, the landing calls, the reveals, the wire packets and the recordings all have a still path under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and every recording carries a Pause/Play control. There are no libraries, no Three.js and no WebGL: static HTML, one stylesheet and two small modules.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Two themes from one token set, switched by `light-dark()` and a stored toggle; show bands force the dark scheme.
- Archivo variable, expanded and heavy for headlines. JetBrains Mono only for tool calls, commands and YAML.
- Buttons are cue-light keys with a lamp dot.
- State colour appears only as round ring-and-core lights, lamp dots, progress rails and state-coloured text or fills.
- Hairline-ruled lists (run-sheet, setup steps, handoff steps, FAQ) carry most sections.
- Real recordings in a plain CSS phone frame, synced to a terminal card of the agent's real calls.

## Colors

A neutral grey stage with three saturated state colours and one indigo accent, all inherited from the Mac app, plus fixed show-band darks.

### Primary
- **Rig Indigo** (`accent`): the fill of the primary key ("Download for Mac"), text selection and the skip link. Its ink twin `accent-ink` carries links, focus rings, YAML keys, the `>` prompt mark in the agent card, the main setup step number and lamp dots on hover.

### Secondary
- **Agent Purple** (`agent`): the agent card's title lamp, the ring-and-core dot of the call that is running, the active task's progress rail, the agent lamp beside each use-case answer, the "Agent driving" role light, the hand-back handoff step and the live wire packet in How it works. `agent-ink` colours call numbers and YAML strings.
- **You Indigo** (`you`): the role light and handoff step light when the person is driving.

### Tertiary
- **Needs-You Orange** (`handoff`): the "Waiting for you" role light, the "agent asks" handoff step light and the dash before each limit in Privacy and limits. `handoff-ink` colours the Beta tag. Light mode uses burnt orange so the colour also works as text.
- **Pass Green** (`ok`): the dot before the agent's answer in the agent card, `PASS` lines in the terminal page and the copy button after a copy. It is the one saturated colour outside the app's control set.

### Neutral
- **Stage Grey / Stage Black** (`ground`): the page background, and the dark ground of the show bands.
- **Deep Ground** (`ground-deep`): the alternate band behind the Handoff and Flows sections, bounded by `line` rules.
- **Prompt-Book Paper** (`paper`, `paper-2`): the few raised surfaces (hub card, prompt book, command fields) and secondary fills (copy button).
- **Graphite Ink** (`ink`, `ink-2`, `ink-3`): headings and strong text, body and secondary text, idle lamps, idle call dots, the `phonedock ·` namespace in calls and quiet marks.
- **Hairlines** (`line`, `line-2`): section rules, list rules, borders on keys and panels. `line` also fills inline code and hover backgrounds.
- **Booth Light** (`booth`, `pool`): cool, low-alpha light. `booth` glows from the top of the flow page in dark mode; `pool` is the closing section's light pool.
- **Show Darks** (`scrim`, `term-glass`, `phone-black`, `phone-rim`): fixed in both themes. `scrim` is the night black behind the devices stage and the base of every scrim gradient (`rgb(7 7 11 / α)`); `term-glass` is the agent card's translucent body; `phone-black` and `phone-rim` build the phone frame.

### Named Rules
**The State Colour Rule.** Purple, indigo and orange mean agent, you and needs-you everywhere on the page. Don't use them for decoration. Cool booth light (the indigo pool behind the hero phone, `booth`, `pool`) is lighting, not state, and stays low-alpha.

**The Shared Palette Rule.** Control colours match `design/DESIGN.md` exactly, per theme. New state colours start in the app's guide, then come here.

**The Show Band Rule.** The hero, the use cases and the handoff force `color-scheme: dark` in both themes, so the footage always sits on show black. The nav reads in the dark scheme over the hero and follows the page theme once scrolled.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo variable, self-hosted (`site/fonts/archivo-var.woff2`, weight 400 to 850, width 75% to 125%), with the system sans as fallback.
**Body Font:** Archivo at normal width.
**Label/Mono Font:** JetBrains Mono variable, self-hosted (`site/fonts/jetbrains-mono-var.woff2`).

**Character:** Archivo pushed wide and heavy reads like stencilled stage signage at headline size and stays plain and readable at body size. JetBrains Mono marks anything a person or agent would actually type or receive.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, width 110%): the hero headline only, about 9.5em wide at most; clamp(2.125rem, 9.4vw, 2.75rem) below 820px. The closing "Give your agent a phone" heading uses the same voice a little wider (116%) at clamp(2.5rem, 5.2vw, 4.75rem).
- **Headline** (760, width 112%): section headings. Balanced wrapping. Over the handoff mood image it carries a soft dark text shadow.
- **Lede** (620, width 104%): the one large statement that opens "What it is", capped at 44ch.
- **Quote** (520): the prompt in each "More prompts to try" row.
- **Run prompt** (520): the quoted prompt in each use-case run; the plain-language Instagram line drops to 480 in `ink-2`.
- **Title** (680 to 700, width 108%, 1 to 1.25rem): setup step titles, device names, use-case app names, "More prompts to try", Privacy and limits subheads.
- **Sub** (420): the line under each section heading, in `ink-2`, capped at 38rem. The hero sub runs at clamp(1.0625rem, 1.25vw, 1.1875rem), 34rem wide.
- **Body** (420, line-height 1.6): running text. Strong text is 650.
- **Small** (0.9375rem): captions, use-case answers, run-sheet notes, device notes, setup notes. The agent card's prompt and answer use this size at 560 and 420.
- **Caption** (0.8125rem): the demo note under the agent card and the call count beside each answer.
- **Tab** (620, width 104%): the three task tabs above the agent card.
- **Label** (660, 0.75rem, width 80%, letter-spacing 0.1em, uppercase): only the "Agent" and "Devices" column labels in the How it works diagram. The Beta tag uses the same condensed caps at 0.6875rem.
- **Key** (620, width 104%): text on keys.
- **Mono** (450, 0.86em): inline tool calls and commands. Calls in the agent card run at 0.75rem; code blocks run at up to 0.875rem with line-height 1.75. Clock digits are tabular.

### Named Rules
**The Wide Headline Rule.** Headlines use Archivo at 110% to 116% width with negative tracking (-0.028em to -0.035em). Body text stays at 100% width.

**The Typed Text Rule.** JetBrains Mono appears only for tool calls (`get_ui`, `tap`, `request_human`), shell commands, the agent card's call lines and clock, and flow YAML or terminal output. Prose, prompts, answers, labels and buttons never use it.

## Layout

Content sits in a centred wrap of up to 1240px with side gutters of 56px, 40px below 1080px and 20px below 820px. Sections are separated by vertical padding of 112px (96px below 1080px, 72px below 820px), with the opening "What it is" (128px top) and the closing call (144px) slightly taller. Each section opens with a heading block up to 44rem wide and 40 to 64px of space below it.

The hero is full-bleed, at least max(700px, 100svh) tall, on a 1480px wrap. Copy sits left in a column up to 36rem; on the right the demo pairs the agent column (task tabs, agent card, note; clamp(300px, 25vw, 380px) wide) with the phone, whose frame is clamp(250px, 32vh, 324px) wide so it stands about 70vh tall. Below 1200px the hero stacks: copy, then the demo centred. Below 820px the demo is one column with the phone first. At 600px and below it reorders to tabs, phone, card and note, each up to 440px, so the whole demo fits one view: the card's prompt clamps to two lines and the log shrinks to 128px.

Use cases are four runs in a subgrid row (app, prompt, phone clip, answer) so the parts line up across runs; two across below 1080px, and below 820px a horizontal swipe carousel with scroll snap, cards min(80%, 320px) wide and the next one peeking in. "More prompts to try" follows as a ruled run-sheet.

The handoff band opens with a mood image behind its heading (up to 620px tall, masked in from the left), then the three steps run across in three columns above the full-width recording. Below 820px the mood image sits above the heading and the steps stack. Devices opens with a full-width dark stage holding the real PhoneDock window up to 908px wide, a caption under it, then the four device kinds in four columns (two below 1080px and 820px, one below 480px).

Other sections are ruled grids: a three-column role row, a three-column call list, a three-part run-sheet (kind, prompt, note), the 1:1 prompt book, 4:4:4 Privacy and limits and 4:8 FAQ. Spacing steps are 8, 12, 16, 20, 28 and 40px, with gaps that scale through `clamp()`.

### Named Rules
**The Inner Padding Rule.** A bordered or filled container keeps at least 8px of inner padding between its edge and its content.

**The One View Rule.** On a phone the hero demo (tabs, phone, card, note) fits one viewport, so a call lands in view while the screen changes.

## Elevation & Depth

The page is mostly flat. Depth lives in the show bands: a defocused photograph under a left scrim and fine SVG grain in the hero, a masked mood image behind the handoff heading, and a blurred, scrimmed image behind the real app window in Devices. The hero phone stands in a soft cool light pool. On top of that a handful of objects lift off the ground with long, soft, negatively spread shadows, each with a hairline or rim edge. The hero copy, lists and grids never cast shadows.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card lift** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 0 rgb(255 255 255 / 0.05) inset, 0 40px 80px -40px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.9)`, with a 16px backdrop blur): the agent card.
- **Phone lift** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px rgb(255 255 255 / 0.06), 0 0 0 2px #2a2a31, 0 0 0 3px #0b0b0e, 0 44px 70px -40px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.9)`): the phone frame, a metal rim around a black slab.
- **Window lift** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgb(255 255 255 / 0.12), 0 0 0 7px rgb(10 10 15 / 0.55), 0 48px 90px -30px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.85)`): the real app window on the devices stage.
- **Hub lift** (`box-shadow: 0 24px 60px -36px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.5)`): the PhoneDock card in the How it works diagram.
- **Book lift** (`box-shadow: 0 40px 90px -50px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.45)`): the open prompt book in Flows.
- **Media lift** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px var(--line-2), 0 40px 100px -50px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.55)`): the handoff recording.

### Named Rules
**The Lit Is Live Rule.** Light marks what is active: the running call wears the purple ring, the active task's rail fills purple, the lit handoff step follows the recording, the phone in play stands in a light pool, and the closing pool brightens when it scrolls into view.

## Shapes

Corners are gently rounded and scale with the object: 2px for progress rails, 6px for tags and inline code, 8px for nav links and copy buttons, 9px for nav buttons and the card's Pause, 10px for keys and media controls, 12px for command fields and the app window, 14px for the handoff video, 16px for the agent card and prompt book, 18px for the hub card and devices stage. The phone frame is proportional: bezel 3% of its width, outer corners 15%, screen corners 12%, with a black status strip carrying a drawn punch-hole camera (a plain black strip on the iPhone clip). Cue lights, lamps, role lights, step lights and the running-call dot are rings with a filled core (a 1.5px border around a padded content-box fill). Borders are 1px hairlines in `line` or `line-2`. The FAQ disclosure is a drawn chevron. The flow page carries one hand-drawn pencil bracket as an inline SVG.

## Components

### Buttons (Keys)
Keys look like the buttons on a stage manager's cue desk. Each key has a lamp dot before its label.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (10px), 48px tall, padding 0 20px 0 16px, 1px `line-2` border on a 72% `paper` wash.
- **Lamp:** a 12px ring with a 2px inset dot, idle in `ink-3`. On hover or focus the lamp lights in `accent-ink` and the border darkens to `ink-2`. Pressing moves the key down 1px.
- **Primary:** `accent` fill, white text, a translucent white lamp that turns solid white on hover. The fill lightens by mixing 12% white on hover.
- **Disabled:** 45% opacity, no press movement.
- **Mobile:** below 480px keys in an action row share the width and centre their labels, and "View on GitHub" shortens to "GitHub".

### Agent Card
The site's own dark terminal card, titled Claude Code. It is not a copy of any app's UI.
- **Bar:** a purple ring-and-core lamp, "Claude Code" in 650 `ink`, a clock reading "at 0:13" in tabular mono that shows the time in the original recording, and a quiet Pause/Play button (32px, 9px corners, `line` fill on hover).
- **Prompt:** a `>` mark in `accent-ink` and the real prompt, typed at 120 characters a second with a blinking block caret. Its row is sized for three lines so the card never jumps.
- **Log:** the real PhoneDock calls as `phonedock · name(args)` (namespace in `ink-3`, name in `ink`, args in `ink-2`), each landing at its time in the clip with a 0.42s rise. An idle call has a 7px `ink-3` dot; the running call wears an 11px purple ring-and-core dot. Then "N more calls, then the answer" with an empty dot, and the answer typed at 75 characters a second beside a green dot. The log is 236px tall (208px below 820px, 128px at 600px), fills from the top, and once full pins to the newest line with a 64px fade at the top.
- **Tabs:** three task tabs above the card, each with a 2px progress rail that fills as its run plays; the active rail is purple.
- **Reduced motion:** the poster frame and the whole transcript show at once, held, with Play to start.

### Phone Frame
A plain black slab with no brand marks, drawn in CSS, holding a real recording. The recording sits under a black status strip with a punch-hole camera, because the clips start below the phone's own status bar. In the hero three clips cross-fade (0.5s) in turn, each loaded just before its turn. In the use cases each phone is 240px wide (220px in the mobile carousel) with its own Play/Pause control.

### Use-Case Run
One column of the use-case row: app name over a top rule, the quoted prompt, the phone clip with Play/Pause, then the answer. The answer row has a purple agent lamp, "Claude Code" and the call count ("9 PhoneDock calls") pushed right, and the first line of the agent's answer in `ink-2`. The iPhone run has no agent and no lamp.

### Ruled Lists
The main reading pattern. Rows are separated by 1px hairlines with a closing rule under the last row.
- **Run-sheet (More prompts to try):** kind, quoted prompt, note, with 28px rows.
- **Handoff steps:** three columns across the recording's width, each with a 14px step light. The lights follow the handoff recording's timeline (asks, take over, hand back) in orange, indigo and purple.
- **Setup steps:** large condensed-wide numerals (800, 2rem, width 120%) in a 64px column. The main step's numeral is indigo.
- **FAQ:** native `details` rows with a drawn chevron that turns on open. Summaries go indigo on hover.

### Recordings
The handoff video sits in a 14px-rounded frame with the media lift at full width. A Pause/Play control (32px, 10px corners, dark translucent glass with 8px blur) sits 12px in from the bottom-right corner of every clip. Videos are muted, play only while on screen, wait for Play under reduced motion, and carry descriptive labels.

### Devices Stage
A dark 18px-rounded stage (`scrim`) with the real PhoneDock window as the subject, over a heavily blurred image of phones on stands under a scrim that darkens toward the bottom. A centred caption sits under the stage; below 820px it goes left-aligned and the corners tighten to 14px.

### Command Fields
Copyable commands and prompts in a 12px-rounded `paper` field with a `line-2` border and padding 14px 14px 14px 20px. A 36px copy button on `paper-2` reads Copy, then Copied in green for 2.2s, or "Press ⌘C" with the text selected if the clipboard is blocked. The prompt variant sets its text in Archivo because a prompt is plain language for the agent to read.

### Prompt Book
An open two-page book in Flows on `paper` with 16px corners and the book lift. The left page holds the YAML flow with a `booth` glow from the top, a pencil bracket and margin notes on its right. The right page holds terminal output with a gutter shadow along the spine. Below 820px the pages stack, the bracket hides, and the notes move under the code behind a dashed rule.

### Navigation
A fixed 64px bar, transparent and in the dark scheme over the hero. After 24px of scroll it takes a 90% `ground` wash with a 14px backdrop blur and a bottom hairline, and follows the page theme. Links are `ink-2` at 0.9375rem with 8px-rounded hover fills in `line`. The theme toggle and GitHub link are 36px outlined buttons with 9px corners. Links hide below 1080px, and the GitHub label hides below 480px.

### Tags and Lights
- **Beta tag:** condensed caps (0.6875rem, width 85%), a currentColor border, `handoff-ink` text.
- **Role lights:** the 14px ring-and-core light, one per role in "What it is".

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every colour from the custom properties on `:root` and give each new token a light and dark value through `light-dark()`; only show-band darks are fixed.
- **Do** keep agent purple, you indigo and needs-you orange tied to agent, person and help-needed states, matching `design/DESIGN.md`.
- **Do** make buttons as keys: a lamp dot, 10px corners, 48px height, hairline border, and the indigo fill only for the primary action.
- **Do** set headlines in Archivo at 110% to 116% width, 760 to 800 weight, with negative tracking.
- **Do** keep JetBrains Mono for tool calls, commands, YAML and terminal output.
- **Do** keep at least 8px of inner padding in any bordered or filled container.
- **Do** give every moving element a reduced-motion path and every video a visible Pause/Play control.
- **Do** let real footage carry every claim: screen recordings, the agent's own calls and answers, frames of the real app. Mark each shipping raster's origin in its sidecar JSON.
- **Do** keep AI-generated images to mood: defocused, scrimmed or masked, behind real footage or a heading, and credited in the footer.

### Don't:
- **Don't** present an AI image as a recording or let it carry a claim about what the product did.
- **Don't** name or show the real phone's brand or model; the phone frame stays a plain black slab.
- **Don't** crop footage so a privacy blur becomes the subject.
- **Don't** build fake product UI (drawn screens, mock phones, a 3D stage); show the real thing.
- **Don't** use the state colours as decoration, gradients or section tints.
- **Don't** set prose, labels or buttons in JetBrains Mono.
- **Don't** add hard offset shadows. Lifted objects use the long, soft, negatively spread shadows listed above with a hairline or rim edge.
- **Don't** use front-of-house theatre imagery (curtains, masks, velvet, marquee bulbs).
- **Don't** put a small uppercase label above a section heading. Condensed caps are kept for diagram column labels and the Beta tag.
